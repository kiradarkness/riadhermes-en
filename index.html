Staying in Marrakech at Riad Hermès

Staying in a riad in Marrakech means choosing a different pace. Behind the ochre walls of the medina, the bustle fades and time slows down. Riad Hermès is the kind of place you get to know little by little, like a well-kept secret, and are sorry to leave.

Our riad in Marrakech, rooted in the Moroccan way of life

Riad Hermès follows the tradition of the Moroccan townhouse: architecture that looks inward, a hushed atmosphere, and a careful balance of natural materials, light and quiet. Every space has been designed to feel balanced and right, with none of the anonymity of a standard hotel. Our riad in Marrakech is a place to retreat to: a guest house that welcomes you as a guest, not just somewhere to sleep.

Our riad in Marrakech, ideally located for exploring the city

In the heart of the medina, Riad Hermès is a riad in Marrakech from which you can explore the city on foot, through the narrow lanes, the souks and the city’s best-known sights. After a day out exploring, coming back to the calm of the riad is a real pleasure. It is a place to experience Marrakech to the full, without having to choose between being in the thick of it and enjoying some peace and quiet.

A riad in Marrakech made for peace, quiet and switching off

Staying at our riad in Marrakech means giving yourself a proper break. Everything here encourages you to slow down: the silence, the sense of space, the quiet corners to rest in and the feeling of being cut off from the noise outside. At Riad Hermès we favour understated luxury built on simplicity, attentive service and calm, for a truly restful stay.

Planning your stay in Marrakech

01. How do I book my stay at Riad Hermès?

To book your stay at Riad Hermès, simply send us a booking request directly through our website.

Our team will get back to you quickly to confirm your stay in Marrakech.

02. Can we throw a party at the riad?

Yes, you can throw a party at Riad Hermès! The riad has a powerful sound system, and we can organise your wedding, birthday, New Year’s Eve party, honeymoon, seminar and more. To find out more, take a look at our exclusive-use service or contact us directly at contact@riadhermes.com

03. Can I give someone a stay as a gift?

You can give someone a stay at the riad as a gift voucher. To send us your request, just use the button below.

Personalised gift voucher
04. Can you pick us up from the airport?

Of course. Our driver will be waiting for you in the airport arrivals hall with a Riad Hermès sign. Our vehicles are spacious and air-conditioned, so you arrive relaxed.
